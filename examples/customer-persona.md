# Customer Persona

## Persona: Fitness Studio Operations Manager

### Profile

The customer is an operations manager at a growing fitness studio with multiple trainers and an increasing number of active members.

They are responsible for coordinating daily operations, supporting staff, monitoring member engagement, and ensuring that the team uses the SaaS platform consistently.

### Customer Goals

- Centralize member and operational information.
- Improve team adoption of the platform.
- Reduce manual administrative work.
- Increase member engagement and retention.
- Help staff use the platform consistently in their daily workflows.

### Current Challenges

- Different team members use the platform in different ways.
- Some features are underused because staff are not sure how they fit into their workflows.
- The customer has limited time for additional training.
- Manual processes still exist outside the platform.
- It is difficult to identify which platform activities are directly contributing to member engagement.

### Customer Signals

The customer may show the following signals during onboarding:

- Questions about which features the team should use first.
- Low or inconsistent activity from some team members.
- Repeated requests for clarification about workflows.
- Limited use of advanced features.
- Interest in improving member engagement but uncertainty about how the platform can support that goal.

### First-Value Milestone

The first-value milestone is reached when the team successfully completes a core workflow in the platform and uses it consistently without requiring repeated support.

### Learning Priorities

The customer should learn first:

1. The core workflow required for daily operations.
2. How different team members should use the platform.
3. Which features directly support member engagement.
4. How to monitor meaningful usage and identify gaps.
5. Where to find relevant resources and support.

### Customer Success Opportunities

Customer Success should focus on:

- Clarifying the customer's desired outcomes.
- Prioritizing the most relevant features instead of presenting the entire platform.
- Providing role-based onboarding and enablement.
- Monitoring adoption signals and identifying friction.
- Connecting product usage to the customer's operational and business goals.

### Persona Insight

This customer does not necessarily need more product information.

They need clarity about how the product fits into their existing workflows and how consistent usage can contribute to measurable customer and business outcomes.
