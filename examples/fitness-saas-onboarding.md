# Fitness SaaS — Customer Onboarding Journey

## Scenario

A fictional fitness SaaS has just acquired a new customer: a small gym with 5 coaches and approximately 300 members.

The gym's main goal is to use the platform to centralize member engagement, automate follow-ups, and increase retention.

## Customer signals

During the initial conversation, the customer mentions:

- They currently manage member communication manually.
- Coaches have different levels of familiarity with the platform.
- They are concerned about adoption across the coaching team.
- They want to see measurable results within the first 30 days.

## AI-assisted interpretation

Based on these signals, the onboarding approach should prioritize:

1. A short, role-based onboarding experience rather than a generic product tour.
2. Early training focused on the workflows the coaches will use most.
3. A simple first-value milestone within the first two weeks.
4. Proactive follow-up with users who show low engagement.
5. A 30-day review focused on adoption, obstacles, and measurable outcomes.

## Proposed journey

### Phase 1 — Understand

Identify the customer's goals, team structure, current workflow, and potential adoption barriers.

### Phase 2 — Activate

Guide the customer through the minimum set of actions required to reach their first meaningful outcome.

### Phase 3 — Enable

Provide targeted training and resources based on each user's role and level of familiarity.

### Phase 4 — Adopt

Monitor usage signals and proactively address friction before it becomes disengagement.

### Phase 5 — Review

Use a 30-day check-in to evaluate progress, gather feedback, and identify opportunities for deeper adoption.

## Why this approach

The objective is not simply to help the customer learn how to use the product.

It is to connect learning, product adoption, and customer outcomes.

AI can support this process by helping identify patterns in customer feedback and usage signals, then suggesting where the journey could be personalized or adjusted.

## Learning outcome

This example demonstrates how customer information can be translated into a structured onboarding and adoption strategy using an AI-assisted approach.
