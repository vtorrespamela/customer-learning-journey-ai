# Customer Journey Map

## From Onboarding to Adoption

This journey maps the customer experience from the moment they start onboarding to the point where they consistently use the product to achieve value.

| Stage | Customer Goal | Customer Signal | CS Action | Learning / Enablement | Success Signal |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Discover | Understand whether the product fits their needs | Questions about use cases and capabilities | Clarify goals and expected outcomes | Provide relevant resources, not a full product tour | Clear success criteria |
| 2. Onboard | Get started with confidence | Initial setup activity | Guide implementation and remove friction | Role-based onboarding | First meaningful action completed |
| 3. Activate | Experience the first value | Early feature usage | Identify blockers and reinforce the right workflows | Targeted guidance based on usage | First-value milestone reached |
| 4. Adopt | Make the product part of the daily workflow | Repeated usage of priority features | Monitor adoption and proactively address friction | Just-in-time learning and support | Consistent usage |
| 5. Expand | Get more value from the platform | Increased usage or new needs | Identify expansion and referral opportunities | Introduce relevant advanced capabilities | Broader product adoption |
| 6. Advocate | Share value and recommend the product | Positive feedback and strong engagement | Capture customer voice and strengthen relationship | Share advanced resources and best practices | Referral, advocacy, or strong NPS |

## Personalization Layer

The journey should not be identical for every customer.

Customer information can be used to personalize:

- Onboarding pace
- Learning format
- Training depth
- Communication frequency
- Recommended resources
- Adoption milestones
- Proactive interventions

## AI Opportunity

AI can support the CSM by analyzing customer feedback, call notes, usage signals, and learning interactions to identify patterns such as:

- Where customers commonly get stuck
- Which learning resources may be most relevant
- Which customers may need proactive support
- Which workflows are associated with stronger adoption
- Where the onboarding journey could be improved

AI should support prioritization and personalization while keeping customer context and human judgment at the center of the process.

## Core Principle

A successful customer journey is not simply a sequence of product interactions.

It is a progression from:

**Understanding → Learning → Activation → Adoption → Value → Growth**

The role of Customer Success is to help customers move through that progression with as little friction as possible.
