# Customer Feedback Analysis

## Scenario

A SaaS customer has completed onboarding but is showing low product adoption after the first two weeks.

During a check-in, the customer says:

> "The platform looks useful, but my team is still doing things the old way. We watched the training, but people are not sure which features they should actually use every day."

## Initial customer signals

- Training was completed.
- Product adoption remains low.
- The team understands the platform at a general level but lacks confidence in daily workflows.
- Existing habits are competing with the new product.
- The customer has not yet reached consistent first value.

## AI-assisted interpretation

The feedback suggests that the main problem is not a lack of training completion.

The stronger signal is a gap between **learning** and **behavioral adoption**.

The customer may know how the product works without knowing how to incorporate it into their existing workflow.

## Customer Success response

Instead of repeating the original product training, the CSM could:

1. Identify the two or three workflows that matter most to the customer's business.
2. Create role-specific guidance around those workflows.
3. Set a short-term adoption goal for the team.
4. Schedule a targeted follow-up to identify friction.
5. Monitor whether usage increases after the intervention.

## Personalized learning intervention

The next learning experience should be based on the customer's actual workflow rather than the full set of product features.

For example:

**Before:**  
"Here are all the things the platform can do."

**After:**  
"Here are the three things your team needs to do every day, and how to do them efficiently in the platform."

## Success signals

The intervention would be considered successful if the customer demonstrates:

- Increased usage of the priority workflows.
- Greater confidence among team members.
- Reduced reliance on previous manual processes.
- Progress toward the customer's stated business goal.
- Consistent product usage beyond the initial onboarding period.

## Key insight

Customer education should not be measured only by whether a customer completed training.

The more meaningful question is:

**Did the learning experience help the customer change behavior and move closer to value?**

This is where Customer Success, Learning & Enablement, and AI-assisted personalization can work together.
