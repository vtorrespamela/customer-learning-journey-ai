# AI-Assisted Onboarding Output

## Customer Input

**Customer:** Pulse Fitness Studio

**Profile:** Growing fitness studio with 300 active members and 5 coaches.

**Primary goal:** Increase team adoption of the SaaS platform and make it part of the team's daily workflow.

**Main challenges:**
- Staff members continue using old processes
- Team members are unsure which features they should use regularly
- Staff have limited time available for training

**Product familiarity:** Basic

**Preferred learning approach:** Short, practical guidance with examples connected to daily tasks

**Engagement signals:** Low product adoption after onboarding

## AI Analysis

Based on the customer information, the main adoption barrier is not a lack of product information.

The main barrier is the gap between product training and the team's daily workflow.

The team received onboarding and training, but employees are still unsure how to apply the platform to their specific responsibilities.

## Recommended Onboarding Approach

The onboarding experience should focus on practical adoption rather than introducing additional product features.

### Priority 1: Connect the product to daily workflows

Identify the team's most common operational tasks and map the relevant platform features to those tasks.

### Priority 2: Reduce learning friction

Provide short, role-specific learning resources instead of broad feature training.

### Priority 3: Create an adoption routine

Encourage the team to use selected core features consistently before introducing additional functionality.

### Priority 4: Monitor engagement

Track early usage signals to identify whether the team is adopting the recommended workflows.

## Personalized Learning Plan

### Week 1 — Understand

- Review the team's current workflow
- Identify the main adoption barriers
- Select the core features most relevant to daily operations

### Week 2 — Apply

- Introduce practical use cases
- Provide short role-specific guidance
- Encourage staff to use the selected features during normal operations

### Week 3 — Reinforce

- Review usage and engagement signals
- Identify remaining friction
- Provide targeted enablement where needed

### Week 4 — Evaluate

- Review adoption progress
- Identify successful behaviors
- Determine whether additional training or intervention is necessary

## Customer Success Actions

The Customer Success Manager should:

- Monitor product adoption
- Identify users or teams showing low engagement
- Reinforce the connection between product features and customer goals
- Provide targeted enablement rather than repeating generic training
- Use customer signals to determine the next intervention

## Success Metrics

Success could be measured through:

- Increased usage of core platform features
- Increased percentage of staff actively using the platform
- Reduction in reliance on previous workflows
- Improved engagement after onboarding
- Increased confidence among staff using the platform

## Key Insight

The customer does not necessarily need more training.

They need a more relevant learning experience that connects the product to their daily work.

AI can help the Customer Success Manager identify these patterns faster and turn customer information into more targeted onboarding and enablement recommendations.

## Human Role

AI supports the analysis and recommendation process, but the Customer Success Manager remains responsible for understanding the customer context, validating the recommendations, and deciding which actions should be taken.

This example demonstrates how AI can support Customer Success without replacing the human relationship.
